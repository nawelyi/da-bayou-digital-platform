# Product Scope — Version 0.1

Primary reference: the **Da Bayou Sports Bar & Grill — Digital Media & Customer Growth Agreement**.

## Explicit Client Requirements

The agreement includes:

- Professional branding for Da Bayou.
- A professional, mobile-friendly website for phones and tablets.
- A homepage highlighting the restaurant and its atmosphere.
- A complete food and drink menu.
- Food photography and featured products.
- Daily and weekly specials.
- Events and entertainment.
- Sports and Gameday promotions.
- Location, hours, contact details, map, and directions.
- Social media links.
- Customer email signup.
- A Customer Club and QR code leading to an opt-in signup landing page.
- A customer database.
- A newsletter and email marketing system.
- The ability to update promotions, specials, and events.
- Optimization of the Google Business presence.
- Promotional graphics and branding for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

Ongoing management after the initial build is not included in the agreement's build fee and requires a separate service arrangement.

## Current Implementation

- An original visual direction inspired by the editorial energy of Louisiana restaurant websites.
- Responsive navigation and core site sections.
- Official food menu boards in an accessible, expandable gallery.
- Dedicated sections for specials, events, and Gameday.
- Customer Club linked to the public MailerLite signup page with consent.
- A final QR code pointing to the Customer Club.
- Confirmed address and a directions link.
- Official Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok links.
- Technical local SEO, social metadata, favicon, robots instructions, sitemap, and structured data.
- Original AI-generated imagery.
- An approval checklist embedded in the current website preview.
- An Opening Soon presentation with examples of lunch specials, planned business delivery, and seasonal crawfish.

## Recommendations for This Version

The following are project recommendations, not verbatim requirements of the agreement:

- Keep the site lightweight and static for initial review.
- Avoid adding a CMS until the person responsible for updating specials and events is identified.
- Clearly label provisional content until the final official information is received.
